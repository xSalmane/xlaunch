Bluebird is a tool that lets you create a token on the Base blockchain by signing in with your X account. By using the site or launching a token, you accept these terms. If you don't accept them, don't use Bluebird.

1. Tokens are speculative

Tokens launched on Bluebird have no intrinsic value, no issuer behind them and no promise of any return. Their price can drop to zero at any moment. Nothing on this site is investment, financial, legal or tax advice. Only put in what you can afford to lose entirely.

2. How it works

  • Each token has a fixed supply of 1,000,000,000. The whole supply goes into a Uniswap v3 pool at launch: nobody, including Bluebird and the creator, receives tokens.
  • The liquidity position stays in the Bluebird contract forever and cannot be withdrawn.
  • Every trade pays a 1% pool fee. Half of the fees collected go to the token's creator, the rest to Bluebird.
  • Creator earnings are held under the creator's X account and can be claimed to a wallet. If you lose access to your X account, you may lose access to unclaimed earnings.
  • Bluebird pays the gas for launches and claims. We may limit, pause or stop this at any time.
